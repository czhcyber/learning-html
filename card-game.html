How to play
  1. The deck starts with 40 cards (1–10 across four suits: ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣).
  2. Each player is dealt 5 cards. You play as Player 1, and the computer plays as Player 2.
  3. On your turn, click the deck to draw a card, then click one of your cards to play it onto the battlefield.
  4. The AI will then play its own card automatically.
  5. The higher card value wins the round. Suits break ties: ♠ > ♥ > ♦ > ♣.
  6. After both cards are played, the winner is shown and players draw back up if the deck still has cards.
  7. The game ends when the deck is empty and both players have no cards left. The player with more round wins is the champion!
